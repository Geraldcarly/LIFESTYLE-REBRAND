Our Story

Born from Purpose

Founded in 2025 by Quincy — a vision to merge fashion with social impact.

About Lifestyle

Lifestyle was founded in 2025 by Quincy, a young creative with a vision to build more than a fashion brand. What started as a simple idea soon became a purpose—to create a brand that inspires people to express themselves confidently and embrace their individuality without trying to fit into someone else's definition of style.

Growing up, Quincy believed that fashion had the power to tell stories. The way people dress reflects their personality, ambitions, and experiences, making clothing more than just something you wear. This belief became the foundation of Lifestyle: a brand built on authenticity, confidence, and self-expression.

Lifestyle was created for people from all walks of life. It is a community that celebrates creativity, diversity, and the freedom to be yourself. Every collection is thoughtfully designed with a clean, modern aesthetic that blends timeless simplicity with contemporary streetwear, creating pieces that people can wear with confidence every day.

Our signature black and grey identity represents strength, confidence, and simplicity—values that define both the brand and the people who wear it. Rather than chasing short-lived trends, Lifestyle focuses on building a lasting identity that encourages individuals to stand out by staying true to themselves.

Beyond fashion, Lifestyle believes that true success is measured by the positive impact a brand has on the people around it. Giving back has been part of the vision from the very beginning. Through community initiatives, collaborations, and future outreach programs, Lifestyle aims to support young creatives, uplift local communities, and create opportunities that inspire growth and hope. We believe that when a community grows stronger, everyone benefits.

Today, Lifestyle continues to grow with the same mission that inspired its beginning: to empower a generation of dreamers, creators, and everyday individuals to wear their confidence, tell their story, and make a meaningful difference in the world around them.

The Founder

Quincy

Quincy founded Lifestyle with a simple belief: what you wear tells the world who you are. Growing up in Tanzania, he saw how fashion could empower communities and spark positive change. In 2025, he turned that belief into a brand — one that doesn't just sell clothes, but builds a movement of confident, creative, and responsible individuals.
